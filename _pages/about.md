I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the [Aerospace Environmental Impacts Simulation (AEIS) group](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/aerospace-environmental-impacts/aeis-team/) within the Department of Aeronautics at **Imperial College London**. 

My research lies at the intersection of **atmospheric science**, **high-performance computing (HPC)**, and **applied computational modeling**. I specialize in developing scalable Lagrangian frameworks and stochastic numerical models to simulate atmospheric processes, contrail-cirrus interactions, and multiphase transport phenomena.

Prior to joining Imperial College London, I completed my Ph.D. in Chemical Engineering at the **University of Manchester** as a recipient of the prestigious [President's Doctoral Scholar Award](https://www.manchester.ac.uk/study/postgraduate-research/funding/presidents-doctoral-scholar-award/). During my doctorate, I developed multiscale finite-volume and pore-scale computational frameworks to model nonlinear transport and chemical kinetics in Solid Oxide Fuel Cells (SOFC).

---

## Research Interests

* **Atmospheric & Contrail Modeling:** Lagrangian particle-based models, contrail-to-cirrus cloud microphysics, stochastic parameterizations of atmospheric gravity waves.
* **High-Performance & Scientific Computing:** Hybrid parallel architectures (MPI, OpenMP, CUDA, Numba), domain decomposition, high-throughput numerical solvers for nonlinear PDEs.
* **Physics-Informed Machine Learning:** Data-driven and physics-informed models (TBNNs, PyTorch), statistical learning, and surrogate modeling for dynamical systems.
* **Multiscale Fluid & Thermal Systems:** Pore-scale and macro-scale transport phenomena, porous media modeling, electrochemical reaction engineering.

---

## Current Highlights & Projects

* **Project CASTLE** (*Contrail Analysis using Stochasticity and Thermodynamic consistency in a Lagrangian Environment*):  
  Architecting a scalable Lagrangian model in C++ and Python leveraging hybrid MPI/OpenMP parallelization on distributed HPC clusters to investigate regional-scale contrail-cirrus interactions and aviation climate impact.
* **Project CLAMP**:  
  High-performance Lagrangian simulation of plume-scale contrail dynamics, heavily optimized with vectorization and Numba compilation.
* **Solid Oxide Fuel Cell Multiscale Modeling**:  
  Integrated pore-scale and cell-scale models for chemical non-equilibrium reforming and electrode microstructure optimization (available on [GitHub /SOFC-project](https://github.com/HamidAbbasi-R/SOFC-project)).
