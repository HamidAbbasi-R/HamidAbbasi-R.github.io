Served as Graduate Teaching Assistant for multiple core chemical and engineering courses from Sep 2022 to Jan 2024:

* **Process Design:** Guided students in interpreting, constructing, and optimizing Piping & Instrumentation Diagrams (P&IDs) and plant safety flowsheets.
* **Engineering Mathematics 2 & 3:** Led problem-solving sessions and tutoring in Linear Algebra, Ordinary Differential Equations (ODEs), Partial Differential Equations (PDEs), Multivariable Calculus, and Complex Analysis.
* **Fundamentals of Thermodynamics:** Instructed students on 1<sup>st</sup> and 2<sup>nd</sup> Law analyses across diverse thermodynamic power and refrigeration cycles.
