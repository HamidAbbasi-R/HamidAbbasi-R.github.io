Delivered specialized lectures and computational workshops from Sep 2023 to Jan 2024:

* **Reaction Systems & Dynamics:** Instructed industrial engineers and researchers on reaction kinetics, chemical equilibrium, and reactor performance.
* **Stochastic Simulation Algorithms:** Taught the theoretical basis and computational implementation of Monte Carlo methods, specifically the **Gillespie algorithm**, to model the stochastic time-dependent behavior of complex reaction networks.
