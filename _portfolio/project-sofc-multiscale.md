* **GitHub Repository:** [HamidAbbasi-R/SOFC-project](https://github.com/HamidAbbasi-R/SOFC-project)
* **Summary:** Developed during Ph.D. research at the University of Manchester, this project provides a parallel finite-volume numerical model in Python for solving coupled nonlinear PDEs across multiple length scales.
* **Core Capabilities:**
  * Direct pore-scale modeling of gas transport and electrochemical reactions in porous anodes.
  * Synthetic 3D microstructure generation mimicking electrode materials with statistical property evaluation.
  * Integration of internal hydrocarbon reforming kinetics accounting for chemical non-equilibrium.
