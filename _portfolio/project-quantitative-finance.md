Explorations in mathematical finance, stochastic calculus, and systematic trading strategies:

* **[Black-Scholes-Model Dashboard](https://github.com/HamidAbbasi-R/Black-Scholes-Model):**  
  Interactive analytical dashboard exploring option pricing models, Greek sensitivities, implied volatility surfaces, and numerical schemes for American options.
* **[Stochastic-Processes & Portfolio Optimization](https://github.com/HamidAbbasi-R/Stochastic-Processes):**  
  Implementation of Modern Portfolio Theory (MPT), efficient frontier generation, and Value at Risk (VaR) estimation under parametric and empirical distributions.
* **Algorithmic Trading & Statistical Strategies:**  
  * [Pair-Arbitrage](https://github.com/HamidAbbasi-R/Pair-Arbitrage): Statistical pairs trading and cointegration testing.
  * [Adaptive-Channel-Strategy](https://github.com/HamidAbbasi-R/Adaptive-Channel-Strategy): Range breakout and trend-following signals.
  * [Zero-Intelligence-Strategy](https://github.com/HamidAbbasi-R/Zero-Intelligence-Strategy): Limit order book simulation and agent-based market dynamics.
  * [ML-Toolbox-Finance](https://github.com/HamidAbbasi-R/ML-Toolbox-Finance): Comparison of Deep Learning, Hidden Markov Models, and Random Forest for time series forecasting.
