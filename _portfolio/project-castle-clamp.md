### Project CASTLE
*Contrail Analysis using Stochasticity and Thermodynamic consistency in a Lagrangian Environment*

* **Overview:** Architected a scalable Lagrangian framework simulating regional contrail-cirrus interactions and their atmospheric radiative impacts.
* **Technology Stack:** C++, Python, MPI, OpenMP on distributed HPC cluster nodes.
* **Key Innovation:** Coupled Lagrangian particle trajectories with stochastic parameterizations of atmospheric gravity waves to simulate atmospheric ice crystal lifecycles with high physical fidelity and thermodynamic consistency.
* **Status:** Developed within the AEIS group at Imperial College London (repo private pending publication; source code available upon request).

### Project CLAMP
* **Overview:** High-performance Lagrangian simulation of plume-scale contrail dynamics.
* **Optimization:** Leveraged advanced array vectorization and Numba JIT compilation, reducing execution runtimes by orders of magnitude compared to legacy simulations.
